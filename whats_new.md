**Auto-continue now actually resumes after a usage limit resets.** A chat stopped by your 5-hour (or weekly) usage limit used to stay parked even after the limit lifted. The cause was a deadlock: "resume" waited for the *"you've hit your limit"* banner to clear, but that banner only clears once a new turn starts — so nothing ever moved. Fixed: auto-continue now watches your **live usage window** and resumes the moment it drops back under the limit. It also freshens your usage data right at the reset so it fires within seconds, never resumes "blind" when usage data is missing, and won't fire on a chat that simply finished normally.
