**What's new since the last stable update**

**A real YOLO mode.** Open the composer's Modes menu (⚡) and pick **YOLO mode** — right under Auto mode — and that chat auto-approves every prompt: file edits, terminal commands, MCP tools. Claude never stops to ask. It's **per-chat**, so turning it on in one chat leaves your others alone. (The old "YOLO" was just a relabel of a mode the editor quietly ignores; this one actually works, because Orbit answers the approval itself.)

**A new "Default" panel.** The gear menu's "Switch model" is now **Default** — one panel where you set what every *new* chat opens on: default **model**, default **mode** (including YOLO), **effort**, and your **Auto-resume** preferences. Change what you like, hit **Save**, and new chats follow it; chats already going keep their own.

**Auto-resume, simplified.** It's now just two switches — resume a chat when your usage limit resets (5-hour or weekly), and resume if a chat errors out.

**"New Session" no longer bounces you back** to the chat you came from a moment after you open a fresh one.

Plus a round of polish: a cleaner composer bottom bar, clearer status and usage display, and a tidier settings menu.
