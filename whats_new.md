**The effort slider is back in the composer.**

A couple builds ago it was removed from the composer bottom bar by mistake — it was only meant to move out of the gear menu (into the new **Default** panel), not disappear from the composer itself. It's restored: the draggable effort slider is back next to the model name for changing the *current* chat's effort on the fly, and the **Default** panel keeps its own for new-chat defaults.
